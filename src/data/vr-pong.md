### VR-Pong Description
VR-Pong is a co-located multiplayer VR web-game which can be simply played with a XR-headset through the web-browser. It was the major part of my master thesis and was used to explore the possibilities of webXR to create co-located multiplayer VR experiences. 

In the game up to 4 players can play a 3D VR-version of the original Pong game against each other. The game was used to investigate the differences between web-based and native approaches and to look for possible limitations of this newer method. 

The project was first displayed at the „Südwest Messe“ in Villingen-Schwenningen in 2025 and is currently still playable at selected events.
